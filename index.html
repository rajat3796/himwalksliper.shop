Our Story

Since its establishment in 2022, Himalayan Walk Slipper has become synonymous with quality, style, and affordability. Officially incorporated as Brand Yellow Star, Himalayan Walk combines innovative technology, cutting-edge design, and customer-centric values to produce a diverse range of products including EVA, PU, and HAWAI. With an unyielding commitment to comfort, durability, and fashion-forward trends, Himalayan Walk has cemented its position as a trusted choice for millions of feet across India.
