About Our Enterprise

Our Story

Established in 2022, Himalayan Walk Sleeper began with a simple yet powerful mission: to redefine everyday comfort and style for millions across India. Officially incorporated under our premium banner Brand Yellow Star, our journey started in the heart of Kangra district, Himachal Pradesh. Over the years, we have grown from a trusted regional manufacturer into a modern footwear provider. We focus closely on engineering gents flip-flops that survive the test of time, tough terrains, and daily rigorous wear, making our brand a reliable household name.

Our Vision & Mission

Our long-term vision is to stand at the pinnacle of the Indian footwear industry by continuously blending innovative designs with high-grade manufacturing techniques. We are deeply committed to maintaining a strict system of quality control, ensuring that not a single slipper leaves our unit without rigorous physical verification. Our mission centers around providing our valued customer base with absolute value for money, combining supreme orthopedic comfort with unparalleled aesthetic appeal.
